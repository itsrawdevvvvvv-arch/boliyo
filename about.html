Built in India, for Indian creators.

Indian creators speak Hinglish — a fluid mix of Hindi and English that every auto driver, college kid and startup founder understands without thinking. Caption tools that learned English first tend to treat it like broken English.

Why we built it

We got tired of manually fixing captions after every upload. Switching between Hindi and English mid-sentence isn't something you can bolt onto a model that learned English first. So Boliyo times every word to the audio, and puts it all behind a simple editor.

How it works

Drop in a video and say which language it is in. Boliyo transcribes it and times every word to the audio. Pick a template or an AI Edits style, fix anything you want, and export. In this build, all of it runs on your own device.

Languages

Eleven languages, plus English.

Captions come out in the language's own script, in English letters the way Hinglish is usually typed, or translated into English.

What it's made of

Small, plain, and fast.

HTML + CSS + JS

No framework, no bundler, no build step. The whole site is static files you can read.

Canvas renderer

Exports draw every caption onto the video frame, then hand you a downloadable file.

On-device

Your video is never uploaded. Captions and projects stay in your browser.

Open

Fork it, restyle it, ship it. MIT licensed.
