Caption minutes

The length of the talk Boliyo writes captions for. A one-minute reel uses one. Retyping or restyling a caption uses none.

Export minutes

The length of every video you export, added up. Exporting a one-minute reel twice uses two, a 4K minute counts as 2½, and tracking the camera uses one.

Before you upload

Frequently asked.

What does it cost?

Trying it is free: 2 caption minutes and 10 export minutes every 30 days, with a small watermark. Any video can be made clean for ₹20, a week of Starter is ₹99, and the monthly plans are ₹349, ₹699 and ₹1,499, GST included.

Can I cancel?

Any time. A month paid once simply ends after 30 days — nothing renews. A monthly plan on UPI Autopay is cancelled from your account in two taps.

What are caption minutes and export minutes?

Every plan has every feature; the plans differ in minutes. Both refill every 30 days, and moving up a plan starts its minutes at once. Editing captions, styles and timing never uses any.

What can I export?

MP4 in 9:16, 16:9, 1:1 or 4:5, in 1080p or 4K, and the captions as an SRT file. Every plan can do all of it; a 4K minute uses 2½ export minutes.
