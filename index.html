Before you upload

Questions, answered.

How does Boliyo work?

Drop in a video and say which language it is in. Boliyo transcribes it and times every word to the audio, usually in under a minute. Pick a template or an AI Edits style, fix anything you want, and export an MP4.

Which languages does it understand?

Hindi, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu and English — or let it detect the language. Captions come out in the language's own script, in English letters (the way Hinglish is usually typed), or translated into English.

Can I change the words after the captions are made?

Yes. Every word can be retyped, retimed, recoloured or made bigger, and splitting or joining lines is a click. The export draws exactly what the preview shows.

What does "Behind you" mean?

Those looks put a word behind the speaker, as if it were part of the scene. They look best when there is some room above your head.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Boliyo runs in the browser. In this build everything happens on your device — your video is never uploaded.

Is it really free to start?

Yes. No card. Free has every feature, with 2 caption minutes and 10 export minutes every 30 days, and its exports carry a small watermark.
